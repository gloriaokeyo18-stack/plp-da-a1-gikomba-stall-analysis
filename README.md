# Wanjiku's Fresh Produce Stall – Performance Analysis

**Analyst:** Gloria Okeyo  
**Cohort:** Data Analytics Cohort

## Project Overview

This project analyses the sales and inventory performance of Wanjiku's fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market over a 14-day period. Wanjiku maintains handwritten daily sales and stock records and needs insights into her business performance, sales growth, inventory requirements and product profitability. Using core Python, this analysis evaluates daily revenue, week-on-week growth, weekday versus weekend sales patterns, inventory reorder requirements and gross margins. The objective is to translate basic business records into practical recommendations that support informed business decisions.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Sales growth:** Total sales were KES 46,450 over 14 days, with a 10.66% increase between Week 1 and Week 2.
2. **Weekend performance:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900, approximately 82.50% higher than average weekday sales of KES 2,685.
3. **Inventory and profitability:** Tomatoes and sukuma were below their reorder levels, while avocado recorded the highest gross margin at 42.86%.
